Mammoth truck wrap rough layouts

These are rough layout sketches placed on the actual truck photos to help choose direction before spending time polishing production art. The goal is a clean partial wrap that reads fast from the road.

Reference photos

Exact side-view truck photo.

Tailgate reference photo.

Concept A

Clean / professional partial wrap

Heavier emphasis on the Mammoth brand, simpler lower-body color sweep, and readable phone + services text. Best fit if you want it to feel established and clean without looking too busy.

  • Large white space preserved
  • Dark green lower band with gold accent
  • Brand-first, less aggressive
Concept B

Bolder / more aggressive partial wrap

Stronger block treatment across the side for more distance impact. Better if you want the truck to feel more dominant on the road and more obviously branded from far away.

  • Stronger side presence
  • Bigger wordmark feeling
  • More visual punch, slightly less subtle
Tailgate

Clean tailgate direction

I’d keep the tailgate simpler than the side: centered logo, strong phone number, and short service line. Tailgates get read quickly at stoplights, so simple usually wins.

  • Big centered logo
  • Phone number made the hero
  • Short services line only
