Privacy policy

How Mammoth Tree Service handles your information.

Effective date: October 9, 2026. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and how text message consent works.

1. Information we collect

When you request a quote or contact Mammoth Tree Service, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, property city or area, service details, uploaded photos, and any other information you choose to send.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to quote requests, review tree service jobs, communicate about scheduling, provide estimates, follow up about requested services, improve our website, and protect against spam or misuse.

3. Text messages and phone communications

If you give permission to receive text messages, Mammoth Tree Service may send SMS messages related to your quote request, scheduling, service updates, or customer support. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or call 334-326-2020.

Consent to receive text messages is not required to request a quote or buy services from Mammoth Tree Service.

4. Sharing of information

Mammoth Tree Service does not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, manage forms, communicate with customers, measure advertising performance, or run our business, but only as needed for those purposes.

SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

5. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This website may use cookies or similar technologies, including tools from providers such as Google, to understand website traffic, improve user experience, and measure ad performance. Those tools may collect information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and general location data.

6. Data security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information submitted through this website. However, no method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may choose not to submit website forms or photos. If you want us to stop texting you, reply STOP. If you want to contact us about information you submitted through the website, call 334-326-2020 or use our contact page.

8. Third-party links

This website may link to third-party websites, including Google review pages or other external services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party sites.

9. Changes to this policy

Mammoth Tree Service may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Mammoth Tree Service at 334-326-2020 or through the website contact form.
